  only secret key (=46D8C06…=, nsaspy <nsaspy@airmail.cc>); git's configured
  identity (N545PY <nsaspy@fedora.email>) has no secret key, and pinentry was
  cancelled/unavailable in this autonomous session. The tag content is fully
  prepared and the human step is one command:

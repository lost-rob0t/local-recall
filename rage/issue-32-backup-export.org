* Acceptance criteria
- Archive inspection reveals no captured text, titles, thumbnails, prompts, or credentials.
- Restore reproduces searchable records after the required key is made available.
- Corrupted, truncated, or wrong-recipient archives fail safely.
- Export and restore operations are audited without logging archive content.

* Design decisions
- Archives copy canonical encrypted envelopes verbatim; nothing is decrypted during backup. The manifest is content-free (versions, count, body digest).
- No active key material is exported: envelopes carry only key-handle references and wrapped per-record data keys.
- Restore requires an empty target unless explicitly overridden; identical duplicates are skipped, conflicting duplicates fail safely; schema-version mismatches fail safely; body digests are verified before any write.
- GPG recipient encryption is optional and implemented with strict argument lists, no shell, bounded timeouts, and no passphrase or key material in archives/logs.
- Export supports full and explicit time-range windows using content-free day buckets.
- Audit events =export_decision= and =restore_decision= carry counts/outcomes only.
